This Privacy Policy applies to the Blick Commute Android application (shown in the app as Blick). It explains what information is processed when you use the app, why it is needed, where it is stored, which service providers receive information, and the privacy choices available to you.

1. Operator

Blick Commute is operated by Blick Labs, an independent software studio based in Stockholm, Sweden.

Privacy contact: contactblicklabs@gmail.com

2. Information Blick Commute processes

Blick Commute does not require a Blick account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, contacts or precise location.

To provide departure, journey and disruption information, the app sends limited service requests over the internet. These can include:

  • station or stop search text;
  • selected site/stop, route, line, direction and transport-mode identifiers;
  • departure, exact-destination journey and disruption request parameters needed to return the requested public-transport information;
  • if you buy or restore Blick Premium, the Google Play product ID and purchase token needed to validate and acknowledge the purchase. The raw purchase token is used for verification but is not stored in Blick's purchase database. Blick stores a SHA-256 fingerprint of the token together with limited purchase-lifecycle information needed to manage the entitlement; and
  • if you activate reviewer access, the reviewer access code you enter, solely so Blick's backend can validate access. The raw code is used transiently for this check and is not persisted by Blick's application; a one-way fingerprint derived from it and a keyed one-way fingerprint derived from the request's network address are used in separate, short-lived rate-limit counters; and
  • technical network information automatically processed when a request reaches the hosting service, such as IP address and basic request logs.

The Basic version of Blick Commute may display banner advertising provided by Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Google states that the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares certain device and usage information for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes. Depending on your device, settings, consent choices and the ad-serving mode, this may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • user-product interactions such as app launches, taps and ad/video interactions;
  • diagnostic information such as app/SDK performance, launch time, hang rate and energy usage; and
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device.

Blick does not request Android permission for precise location. General location may still be inferred by advertising or infrastructure providers from an IP address.

Blick Commute does not send your saved commute routines to Google AdMob for ad targeting, and Blick Labs does not use your station searches, destinations or journey requests to create its own advertising profile or to track your public-transport activity across apps or websites.

Blick Commute does not include a separate general-purpose analytics SDK. Google Mobile Ads may nevertheless process data for advertising-related analytics and measurement as described above.

3. Information stored on your device

Your saved commute routines, one-time events, labels, app preferences, routine runtime state and cached commute information are stored locally on your Android device. The service-request parameters described above are sent as needed to retrieve transport information; your complete saved routines and event titles are not uploaded. Blick Commute does not provide cloud account sync or cloud backup for this information, and the app explicitly excludes its data from Android app backup and device-to-device transfer.

If reviewer access is activated, Blick stores only a local flag recording that reviewer access is active on that installation. The reviewer code itself is not stored on the device. The flag is excluded from Android backup and device-to-device transfer and remains across ordinary app updates until reviewer access is deactivated, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Google's consent and advertising services may also store consent or advertising-related state on your device where permitted or required. You can review applicable advertising privacy choices from Blick's Settings when the privacy-options entry point is required.

You can remove Blick's local information by deleting a routine, clearing Blick Commute's app data in Android settings, or uninstalling Blick. Android also provides controls to reset or delete the device's advertising ID.

4. Backend and service providers

Blick Commute uses a backend service to retrieve and normalize public-transport information. The production backend is hosted by Vercel. Vercel may process limited technical information such as IP addresses and request logs as part of delivering, protecting and operating the service.

If you buy or restore Blick Premium, Blick Commute sends the product ID and purchase token to the Blick backend. The backend passes the token to the Google Play Developer API to verify the current purchase state and acknowledge a valid purchase when required. The raw purchase token is used for that verification request but is not stored in Blick's purchase database. Blick stores a SHA-256 fingerprint of the token together with the product and Google order identifiers, purchase and acknowledgement state, purchase and verification times, quantity, and refund or revocation state in its PostgreSQL database. This durable record is used to manage Premium entitlement, restore purchases, process Google Play lifecycle notifications and prevent duplicate processing. Google Play processes purchase verification, while Blick's hosting and database providers process the stored billing record. Blick Labs does not receive your payment-card details; Google Play handles payment and the purchase record associated with your Google Play account.

Reviewer access does not use Google Play Billing and does not create a purchase. When activation is requested, the entered code is sent over HTTPS to the Blick backend. The backend hashes it and compares the result with a server-side verification digest. Blick's application processes the raw code only for that request and does not write it to application logs or databases. Separate Redis counters use a one-way fingerprint derived from the code and a keyed, domain-separated one-way fingerprint derived from the client network address supplied by Vercel. Neither the raw code nor the raw network address is stored in those counters, which are configured to expire after 60 seconds. Vercel processes the request as Blick's hosting provider, while the Redis provider processes the short-lived counters.

The Basic version uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to provide banner advertisements. Google and participating advertising partners may process or receive advertising, device, interaction and diagnostic information according to the applicable ad-serving mode and your privacy choices. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising privacy information.

Blick Commute also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising/privacy choices where required. In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, this includes the disclosures and consent choices required by Google's EU User Consent Policy.

Public-transport information is obtained from services provided through SL and Trafiklab. Necessary stop, route and journey request parameters may be processed by these services in order to return the requested commute information.

Blick Commute's disruption infrastructure may use shared caching to protect upstream public-transport services from excessive requests. This cache contains public-transport disruption data and operational cache/lock state, not Blick Commute user profiles.

The backend also uses Redis for short-lived technical state that limits purchase-verification requests and protects the service from excessive traffic. This includes counters associated with a SHA-256 purchase-token fingerprint and an overall billing counter, configured to expire after a 60-second window. The counters do not contain raw purchase tokens, saved routines or event titles.

5. How the information is used

The information described above is used to:

  • search for Stockholm public-transport stops;
  • retrieve upcoming departures, exact-destination journeys and relevant disruption information;
  • validate, acknowledge, restore and maintain Blick Premium purchases through Google Play, including processing refund and revocation lifecycle notifications;
  • validate controlled, transaction-free reviewer access to Premium features;
  • provide banner advertising in the Basic version through Google AdMob;
  • support advertising measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention where performed by Google Mobile Ads;
  • operate, protect and troubleshoot the Blick Commute service; and
  • maintain reliability and security.

Blick Labs does not sell personal data. Blick Labs does not independently use your commute routines, station searches, destinations or journey history to build an advertising profile.

Where advertising personalization is permitted and the required consent has been provided, Google and participating advertising partners may use advertising-related data for personalized advertising in accordance with the privacy choices presented through Google's consent system.

6. Advertising and privacy choices

Where required, Blick Commute uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present advertising and privacy choices before requesting an ad.

Depending on your location and applicable law, the consent interface may offer choices such as Consent, Do not consent and Manage options. Choosing not to consent does not prevent you from using Blick Commute.

Depending on the consent signals and available inventory, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized or limited ads, or no ad may be returned. Limited ads restrict the use of personal data for advertising personalization, although information such as IP address can still be required to deliver an ad.

Where Google requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, you can review or change your choices in Blick Commute → Settings → Privacy choices.

Blick Premium is ad-free. While either a verified Google Play Premium entitlement or validated reviewer access is active, Blick Commute does not request banner advertisements. Reviewer access is not a purchase. Google's UMP may still be used to maintain or refresh applicable privacy-choice state.

7. Retention and deletion

Local app information remains on your device until you delete the relevant routine, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Blick Labs does not keep an account-based copy of your routines.

Blick Commute does not intentionally store station searches, route requests or journey requests as a user search history. Limited infrastructure logs may be generated and retained by the hosting provider for operational and security purposes under its applicable service practices.

For Blick Premium, the raw Google Play purchase token is used transiently for verification and is not stored in Blick's purchase database. Blick stores a SHA-256 fingerprint of the token and the limited purchase-lifecycle fields described above. Active purchase records are retained while needed to maintain and restore the Premium entitlement. The retention thresholds for cleanup are 24 months since an inactive purchase record was last updated and 90 days since a lifecycle-notification record was successfully processed. Eligible records are deleted when purchase-verification activity next runs cleanup successfully, including notification handling that invokes verification. There is no independent scheduled cleanup job, so deletion may occur later than these thresholds. Notification records still awaiting processing or marked as failed are not covered by the 90-day cleanup rule.

The raw reviewer access code is not retained by Blick's application after the validation request. Reviewer rate-limit counters derived from the code and a keyed fingerprint of the client network address are configured to expire after 60 seconds; the counters contain neither value in raw form, while provider logs and backups may follow separate retention practices. The local reviewer-access flag remains under the device-storage rules described above. Rotating the server-side verification digest prevents future activation with the previous code but does not remotely revoke reviewer access already active on an installation.

These cleanup rules apply to records in Blick's billing database. Google Play notifications delivered through Google Cloud Pub/Sub can contain purchase tokens. Retention of delivery messages, retries, dead-letter messages, infrastructure logs and provider backups depends on the relevant service and configuration; deleting a Blick database record does not guarantee deletion of those provider-held copies at the same time. The short-lived Redis counters described above have their own configured expiry.

Advertising and consent data processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's applicable service and privacy practices. Blick Labs does not control Google's independent retention periods for data processed by Google Mobile Ads or UMP.

You can clear Blick's local data through Android settings or uninstall the app. You can also reset or delete the Android advertising ID through Android's privacy controls and, where available, change advertising privacy choices from Blick's Settings.

You may request deletion of identifiable purchase records using the privacy contact below, subject to records Blick Labs must retain where required by law or reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security or dispute handling. Refund and revocation notifications mark the associated Premium entitlement inactive.

Because Blick Commute has no Blick account or stable Blick user identifier, Blick Labs will often have no reliable way to connect an infrastructure request log to a specific person. You can still contact us with a privacy or deletion request, and we will act on identifiable data where reasonably possible and legally required.

8. Security

The production app communicates with the Blick backend using HTTPS. Google states that user data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

Blick Commute is designed to minimize commute-related information sent off the device, and it does not request Android permissions for precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but Blick Labs limits its own processing to what is needed to provide, monetize, operate and protect the service.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal data, including rights to information, access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection.

Where applicable, advertising-consent choices can also be reviewed through Settings → Privacy choices in Blick Commute.

To make a request or ask a privacy question concerning information controlled by Blick Labs, contact contactblicklabs@gmail.com.
